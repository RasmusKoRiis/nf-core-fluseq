Assessment rules and interpretation

  • Reported QC: NGS_QC_Sum is preserved. An empty cell means no reported flags; a missing column or NA-like value means unavailable. Source codes: LC = low or missing coverage; FS = frame shift; MS = mixed sites. The existing source rules use coverage <80% and mixed-site sum >3.
  • Additional review checks: any reported flag, missing NGS summary, coverage below 80% or missing/invalid coverage, non-good or missing expected Nextclade QC, or a reported GISAID comment prompts sample review. Coverage-MP takes precedence over Coverage-M. Values outside 0–100% are invalid. No flag is an absence of detected issues, rather than formal sample approval.
  • Upstream limitations: missing frame-shift and mixed-site values can leave the source summary empty. HA and M mixed-site fields may duplicate segment values. This report preserves the source calls; it does not recalculate them. Read counts have no acceptance threshold here.
  • Findings: characterisation and subclade labels describe reported genetic categories. Reassortment conclusions are similarity-screen flags. Drug lookup codes are displayed as reported. These fields do not establish antigenic phenotype, confirmed reassortment or clinical drug susceptibility.
